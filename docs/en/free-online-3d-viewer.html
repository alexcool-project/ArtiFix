Why use an online 3D viewer?

Traditional 3D viewing requires installing heavy software — CAD programs, mesh editors, or proprietary viewers. These tools are often expensive, platform-specific, and slow to start.

An online 3D viewer solves all of these problems. It runs directly in your browser, on any device, without any installation. You upload the file, view it, and close the tab. Nothing more.

Using an online 3D viewer is useful when you want to:

  • Quickly check a 3D file before sending it to a 3D printer
  • Inspect a model without installing CAD software
  • View a 3D file on a computer or tablet that doesn't have your usual tools
  • Share a model with a client who doesn't have specialized software
  • Verify a download before using it in another application

How to view a 3D model online in 4 simple steps

Follow these steps to view your 3D model in less than a minute.

1

Open ArtiFix in your browser

Go to artifix.streamlit.app. No installation, no registration required.

2

Go to the "3D Viewer"

Click on "Viewer 3D" (or "3D Viewer" in English) in the navigation menu on the left.

3

Upload your 3D file

Click "Upload" and select your STL, OBJ, PLY, GLB, GLTF, FBX, 3MF, DAE, WRL or OFF file. You can also drag and drop the file directly into the upload box.

4

Rotate and inspect

Use your mouse (or touch gestures on mobile) to rotate, zoom and inspect the model. The viewer shows the X, Y and Z axes as a reference.
