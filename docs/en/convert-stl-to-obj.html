Why convert STL to OBJ?

STL (Stereolithography) and OBJ (Wavefront Object) are two of the most common formats used in 3D modeling and 3D printing. But they're not interchangeable — each has its strengths and weaknesses.

STL is the standard format for 3D printing. It's simple and universally supported, but it only stores geometry (vertices and faces). No colors, no textures, no material information.

OBJ is more flexible. It also stores color, texture, and material information, and it's better supported by many 3D modeling and animation tools, such as Blender, Maya, and Cinema 4D.

Converting STL to OBJ is useful when you want to:

  • Add color or texture to a 3D printed model
  • Edit the mesh in 3D modeling software that prefers OBJ
  • Share the model with someone using a different workflow
  • Archive the model in a more flexible format
